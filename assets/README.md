🌌 About Gaming Horizon

Gaming does not end when a game ends.

It continues through the people we meet, the communities we build, the ideas we create, the challenges we take on, and the technology that changes how we experience digital worlds.

Gaming Horizon is being built around that larger idea.

Our direction is to develop a connected, browser-first ecosystem where players, creators, developers, competitors, communities, and Explorers can discover experiences, participate meaningfully, create new things, and explore what comes next.

Gaming Horizon is not being built simply to reproduce what already exists.

🌐 Browser-First by Design

Gaming Horizon is being developed around a browser-first philosophy.

The browser is not treated simply as a place to display pages. It is the primary gateway through which Gaming Horizon can connect discovery, community, participation, creativity, competition, and future interactive experiences.

What browser-first means here

Accessible entry — experiences begin through the web without requiring a traditional launcher.

Connected experiences — platform areas are designed to feel like parts of one ecosystem rather than isolated pages.

Modern interaction — interfaces should feel responsive, immersive, clear, and intentional.

Progressive evolution — new capabilities can be introduced as the ecosystem develops.

Cross-device thinking — experiences should remain understandable and usable across supported modern devices and browsers.

<div align="center">

The technology exists to strengthen the journey — not distract from it.

</div>

✦ Why Gaming Horizon

The project is guided by a few deliberate choices:

Discovery over clutter — help people find meaningful experiences without overwhelming them.

Community over passive audiences — design spaces where participation matters.

Creativity over consumption alone — give ideas room to become projects, media, tools, and experiences.

Progress over empty competition — competition should encourage skill, participation, and growth.

Technology with purpose — use technology because it improves the experience, not because it looks futuristic.

Evolution over artificial finality — Gaming Horizon is expected to learn, change, and expand.

🛡️ Security & Trust

Trust should be part of the architecture.

Security vulnerabilities, authentication secrets, API keys, passwords, access tokens, private keys, session tokens, and other sensitive credentials should never be published through public Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, screenshots, or community channels.

A dedicated security policy will be maintained as the repository's security processes are established.
