## ✦ Overview

The Gaming Horizon branding system defines the visual foundation of the project.

It exists to ensure that Gaming Horizon remains recognizable across:

- the website;
- GitHub;
- documentation;
- product interfaces;
- community environments;
- developer resources;
- announcements;
- presentations;
- future applications;
- and other official surfaces.

The purpose of this system is not to force every visual to look identical.

It is to establish a shared identity strong enough that different Gaming Horizon experiences still feel connected to the same universe.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Consistency does not mean repetition.  
> It means recognition.

<a id="visual-direction"></a>

## ✦ Visual Direction

The Gaming Horizon visual language is built around dark atmospheric foundations and controlled energy accents.

### Atmospheric foundation

Typical directions include:

```text
MIDNIGHT BLACK
DEEP CHARCOAL
DARK NAVY
ATMOSPHERIC BLUE
COSMIC DEPTH
```

### Energy accents

Typical accent directions include:

```text
PURPLE
ELECTRIC BLUE
CYAN
VIOLET
SUBTLE MAGENTA
```

These colors should behave like focused energy.

They should not make every element equally bright.

---

## ✦ Lighting

Appropriate lighting may include:

- horizon glow;
- soft bloom;
- aurora-style gradients;
- controlled edge lighting;
- subtle reflections;
- atmospheric particles;
- dimensional shadows;
- restrained neon accents;
- cinematic backlighting.

Lighting should support hierarchy rather than compete with content.

---

## ✦ Geometry

Gaming Horizon may use:

```text
ANGULAR FORMS
HORIZON LINES
DIRECTIONAL GEOMETRY
SUBTLE GRIDS
SPATIAL FRAMES
LIGHT TRAILS
INTERFACE PANELS
LAYERED DEPTH
```

Geometry should improve structure.

It should not exist merely as decoration.

---

## ✦ Effects

Appropriate effects may include:

```text
SUBTLE GLOW
CONTROLLED GRADIENTS
GLASS SURFACES
ATMOSPHERIC DEPTH
SOFT PARTICLES
DIMENSIONAL SHADOWS
RESTRAINED REFLECTIONS
```

Effects should never reduce readability or accessibility.

---

## ✦ What to Avoid

Gaming Horizon should avoid visual presentation that feels:

```text
CHEAP
CHILDISH
GENERIC
OVERLOADED
EXCESSIVELY NEON
UNCONTROLLED CYBERPUNK
CORPORATE-SAAS GENERIC
VISUALLY CLUTTERED
ARTIFICIALLY FUTURISTIC
```

> [!NOTE]
> Futuristic design does not require every surface to glow.

Restraint is part of premium design.

<a id="integrity"></a>

## ✦ Logo Integrity

The logo should remain immediately recognizable.

Avoid:

```text
STRETCHING
COMPRESSING
SKEWING
ROTATING
ARBITRARY RECOLORING
INACCURATE REDRAWING
GEOMETRY MODIFICATION
DESTRUCTIVE CROPPING
EXCESSIVE GLOW
UNCONTROLLED SHADOWS
COVERING THE MARK
CHANGING PROPORTIONS
```

Preserve:

```text
CORRECT PROPORTIONS
RECOGNIZABLE GEOMETRY
CLEAR SPACE
CONTRAST
RESOLUTION
VISUAL BALANCE
```

---

## ✦ Clear Space

The logo should have enough surrounding space to remain visually independent.

Avoid placing it too close to:

- page edges;
- headings;
- other logos;
- interface controls;
- buttons;
- decorative borders;
- unrelated graphics.

---

## ✦ Background Compatibility

Before publishing the logo, verify:

- edge visibility;
- color separation;
- surrounding noise;
- brightness;
- contrast;
- atmosphere;
- readability at intended size.

Extreme glow should not be required to make the logo visible.

---

## ✦ Logo Review Checklist

- [ ] Official asset is being used
- [ ] Correct proportions are preserved
- [ ] No destructive crop is present
- [ ] No arbitrary recoloring is applied
- [ ] No unofficial redesign is used
- [ ] Contrast is sufficient
- [ ] Clear space is appropriate
- [ ] Resolution is suitable
- [ ] Correct file path is used
- [ ] Source asset remains preserved

## ✦ Spacing

Whitespace is part of premium presentation.

Avoid filling every available space with:

```text
Particles
Glow
Icons
Badges
Gradients
Decorative lines
Extra copy
```

Controlled empty space improves focus.

---

## ✦ Glassmorphism

Glass surfaces may support Gaming Horizon where appropriate.

Use:

- sufficient contrast;
- controlled blur;
- readable foreground text;
- subtle borders;
- restrained transparency.

Avoid glass effects that reduce readability.

---

## ✦ Neon

Neon should function as an accent.

Good neon:

```text
CREATES FOCUS
ADDS ENERGY
STRENGTHENS DEPTH
HIGHLIGHTS INTERACTION
```

Poor neon:

```text
COVERS EVERYTHING
REMOVES HIERARCHY
REDUCES READABILITY
CREATES GENERIC CYBERPUNK STYLING
```

---

## ✦ Motion

Motion should communicate:

```text
TRANSITION
DISCOVERY
DIRECTION
ENERGY
DEPTH
PROGRESSION
```

Animation should not exist simply because animation is technically possible.

- support reduced-motion preferences;

- avoid visually overwhelming effects.

CONSISTENCY
FUTURE EDITING

## ✦ Review Framework

Before approving a brand asset, ask:

### Identity

Does it clearly belong to Gaming Horizon?

### Recognition

Does it preserve recognizable identity?

### Purpose

Why does the asset need to exist?

### Quality

Is it suitable for official presentation?

### Consistency

Does it align with the wider system?

### Accessibility

Does it remain understandable and readable?

### Performance

Is it technically appropriate?

### Rights

Can Gaming Horizon legitimately publish it?

## ✦ Truthful Brand Presentation

Branding should not falsely imply:

```text
A partnership exists
A feature has launched
A product is available
An experiment is final
An external company endorses Gaming Horizon
An unofficial visual is official
```

Where needed, use:

```text
IN DEVELOPMENT
EXPERIMENTAL
CONCEPT
COMING SOON
AVAILABLE AT LAUNCH
NOT YET ANNOUNCED
```

Professional presentation depends on trust.

## ✦ Brand Golden Rules

### `01` — Use the official source

When identity accuracy matters, begin with the approved source asset.

### `02` — Preserve proportions

Do not distort the logo.

### `03` — Design around the identity

Do not force the identity to compensate for weak composition.

### `04` — Use effects with control

Glow is an accent, not the entire design.

### `05` — Protect clarity

Visual impact should never destroy usability.

### `06` — Maintain recognition

Different formats should still feel like Gaming Horizon.

### `07` — Preserve source files

Important original assets should remain traceable.

### `08` — Name assets clearly

Avoid confusing duplicate versions.

### `09` — Present truthfully

Branding should strengthen trust.

### `10` — Evolve intentionally

Change should improve the system rather than fragment it.

## ✦ Brand Standard

A Gaming Horizon identity asset should ultimately be:

| # | Standard | Meaning |
|:---:|---|---|
| `01` | **Recognizable** | Clearly Gaming Horizon |
| `02` | **Premium** | Appropriate for official presentation |
| `03` | **Consistent** | Connected to the wider identity |
| `04` | **Clear** | Easy to understand |
| `05` | **Purposeful** | Exists for a meaningful reason |
