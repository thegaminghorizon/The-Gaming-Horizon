<a id="overview"></a>

## ✦ Overview

The Gaming Horizon is built around:

```text
GAMING

DISCOVERY

COMMUNITY

CREATIVITY

COMPETITION

DEVELOPMENT

EXPLORATION
```

A strong ecosystem requires more than good software.

It requires people to be able to participate without unnecessary hostility,
harassment, intimidation, discrimination, manipulation, or abuse.

This Code of Conduct establishes the behavioral standards expected throughout
official Gaming Horizon spaces.

---

## ✦ Our Community Standard

Gaming Horizon should be a place where people can:

```text
ASK QUESTIONS

SHARE IDEAS

REPORT PROBLEMS

DISAGREE

LEARN

BUILD

CREATE

COMPETE

CONTRIBUTE

PARTICIPATE
```

without being attacked for doing so in good faith.

---

## ✦ Core Principle

> **Challenge ideas without attacking people.**

Technical disagreement is normal.

Creative disagreement is normal.

Competitive disagreement is normal.

Hostility toward people is not required for any of them.

<a id="expected-behavior"></a>

## ✦ Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully;
- assume good faith when reasonable;
- criticize ideas without insulting people;
- provide useful context when reporting problems;
- respect personal boundaries;
- respect moderator and maintainer instructions;
- avoid unnecessary disruption;
- keep discussion relevant to the appropriate space;
- help create an environment where people can ask questions safely;
- acknowledge mistakes and correct them;
- respect privacy;
- use reporting systems responsibly;
- follow applicable repository and community rules.

---

## ✦ Respectful Communication

Prefer:

```text
"I disagree with this approach because..."
```

over:

```text
"This is stupid."
```

Prefer:

```text
"Could we simplify this implementation?"
```

over:

```text
"You clearly don't know what you're doing."
```

The goal is not to eliminate disagreement.

The goal is to keep disagreement useful.

---

## ✦ Constructive Criticism

Good criticism should focus on:

```text
THE IDEA

THE CODE

THE DESIGN

THE BEHAVIOR

THE RESULT

THE RISK
```

not personal characteristics.

---

## ✦ Technical Discussions

Technical discussions may involve strong disagreement.

Participants should still:

```text
PROVIDE REASONING

SHARE EVIDENCE

ASK QUESTIONS

ACKNOWLEDGE TRADE-OFFS

AVOID PERSONAL ATTACKS
```

---

## ✦ Code Review Standard

Code review should aim to improve:

```text
CORRECTNESS

SECURITY

ACCESSIBILITY

PERFORMANCE

MAINTAINABILITY

READABILITY

ARCHITECTURE
```

Reviews should not be used to embarrass contributors.

---

## ✦ Feedback Standard

Useful feedback is:

```text
SPECIFIC

ACTIONABLE

RELEVANT

RESPECTFUL
```

---

## ✦ Questions

Questions should be welcomed when asked in good faith.

Do not shame people for:

```text
BEING NEW

ASKING BASIC QUESTIONS

MISUNDERSTANDING DOCUMENTATION

REQUESTING CLARIFICATION
```

Point them toward the right information instead.

---

## ✦ Mistakes

People will make mistakes.

A healthy response is:

```text
IDENTIFY
   │
   ▼
EXPLAIN
   │
   ▼
CORRECT
   │
   ▼
LEARN
```

not:

```text
IDENTIFY
   │
   ▼
HUMILIATE
```

---

<a id="unacceptable-behavior"></a>

## ✦ Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior may result in moderation or enforcement action.

---

## ✦ Harassment

Harassment includes unwanted behavior that repeatedly or seriously targets
another person.

Examples include:

```text
TARGETED INSULTS

INTIMIDATION

THREATS

REPEATED UNWANTED CONTACT

HOSTILE DOGPILING

DELIBERATE HUMILIATION

STALKING-LIKE BEHAVIOR
```

---

## ✦ Discrimination

Discriminatory abuse or exclusion based on protected or personal
characteristics is not acceptable.

---

## ✦ Threats

Threats of violence, harm, retaliation, or coercion are prohibited.

Credible threats may require immediate escalation beyond ordinary moderation.

---

## ✦ Personal Attacks

Disagreement does not justify:

```text
NAME-CALLING

PERSONAL INSULTS

MOCKERY DIRECTED AT AN INDIVIDUAL

DEGRADING COMMENTS
```

---

## ✦ Sexual Harassment

Unwanted sexual comments, advances, or sexualized harassment are prohibited.

Gaming Horizon community spaces are not appropriate places for sexually
explicit conduct.

---

## ✦ Hate Speech

Content that attacks or dehumanizes people based on protected characteristics
is prohibited.

---

## ✦ Doxxing and Private Information

Do not publish another person's private information without authorization.

Examples include:

```text
HOME ADDRESS

PRIVATE PHONE NUMBER

PRIVATE EMAIL

PRIVATE ACCOUNT INFORMATION

LOCATION INFORMATION

PRIVATE IMAGES

IDENTIFYING DOCUMENTS
```

---

## ✦ Impersonation

Do not impersonate:

```text
STAFF

MODERATORS

MAINTAINERS

OTHER MEMBERS

OFFICIAL GAMING HORIZON ACCOUNTS
```

in a way intended to deceive others.

---

## ✦ False Authority

Do not claim to represent Gaming Horizon when you are not authorized to do so.

---

## ✦ Spam

Spam includes:

```text
REPEATED UNSOLICITED PROMOTION

MASS MENTIONS

MESSAGE FLOODING

REPEATED DUPLICATE ISSUES

AUTOMATED DISRUPTION

IRRELEVANT LINK DROPS
```

---

## ✦ Scams and Fraud

Attempts to deceive community members for money, credentials, accounts,
items, access, or other benefit are prohibited.

---

## ✦ Credential Requests

Never ask another member to provide:

```text
PASSWORDS

2FA CODES

RECOVERY CODES

SESSION TOKENS

PRIVATE KEYS

API KEYS
```

through normal Gaming Horizon support or community channels.

---

## ✦ Malicious Links

Do not knowingly distribute:

```text
PHISHING LINKS

MALWARE

CREDENTIAL-STEALING PAGES

DECEPTIVE DOWNLOADS
```

---

## ✦ Deliberate Disruption

Participants must not intentionally interfere with normal project or community
operation.

Examples include:

```text
THREAD FLOODING

TICKET SPAM

BOT ABUSE

ISSUE SPAM

INTENTIONAL OFF-TOPIC DISRUPTION

COORDINATED HARASSMENT
```

---

## ✦ Moderation Evasion

Attempts to bypass moderation actions may result in stronger enforcement.

Examples include:

```text
USING ALTERNATE ACCOUNTS TO EVADE A BAN

RECREATING REMOVED CONTENT REPEATEDLY

EVADING CHANNEL RESTRICTIONS

TARGETING MODERATORS AFTER ENFORCEMENT
```

---

## ✦ False Reports

Reporting systems must not be weaponized against other members.

Knowingly submitting fabricated evidence or malicious reports may itself be
treated as misconduct.

---

## ✦ Public Accusations

When reporting serious misconduct, avoid starting public accusation campaigns.

Prefer:

```text
COLLECT RELEVANT EVIDENCE
        │
        ▼
USE OFFICIAL REPORTING
        │
        ▼
ALLOW REVIEW
```

This protects both reporters and people who may be falsely accused.

---

## ✦ Competitive Misconduct

Where Gaming Horizon competitive systems apply, unacceptable conduct may
include:

```text
CHEATING

RESULT MANIPULATION

COLLUSION

HARASSMENT

IMPERSONATION

EXPLOITING RULES IN BAD FAITH
```

Competition-specific rules may define additional requirements.

See:

```text
docs/COMPETE.md
```

---

## ✦ Repository Misconduct

Repository-specific misconduct may include:

```text
ISSUE SPAM

ABUSIVE CODE REVIEWS

MALICIOUS PULL REQUESTS

DELIBERATE SECRET EXPOSURE

HARASSMENT IN REVIEW COMMENTS

```

Constructive criticism of Gaming Horizon is allowed.

```text
FEATURES

DESIGN

```

## ✦ Moderation Criticism

People may disagree with moderation decisions.

<a id="moderation"></a>

## ✦ Moderation

Moderation exists to protect community participation and project integrity.

It should not be used for:

```text
PERSONAL RETALIATION

WINNING ARGUMENTS

SILENCING RESPECTFUL CRITICISM

FAVORITISM
```

---

## ✦ Moderation Principles

Moderators should aim to be:

```text
CONSISTENT

PROPORTIONATE

EVIDENCE-BASED

RESPECTFUL

PRIVACY-CONSCIOUS

CLEAR
```

---

## ✦ Context Matters

Moderation decisions may consider:

```text
SEVERITY

INTENT

IMPACT

REPETITION

PRIOR WARNINGS

EVIDENCE

IMMEDIATE SAFETY RISK
```

---

## ✦ Not Every Case Is Identical

Moderation should not be reduced to:

```text
SAME WORD
    =
SAME RESULT
```

Context may significantly change the appropriate response.

---

<a id="enforcement"></a>

## ✦ Enforcement

Gaming Horizon may apply different enforcement levels depending on the
situation.

---

## ✦ Level `01` — Guidance

Used for minor or likely accidental problems.

Possible action:

```text
CLARIFICATION

REMINDER

REQUEST TO CHANGE BEHAVIOR
```

---

## ✦ Level `02` — Warning

Used when behavior clearly violates community standards or continues after
guidance.

Possible action:

```text
FORMAL WARNING

CONTENT REMOVAL

RESTRICTION
```

---

## ✦ Level `03` — Temporary Restriction

Used for repeated or more serious violations.

Possible action:

```text
TEMPORARY MUTE

TEMPORARY CHANNEL RESTRICTION

TEMPORARY PROJECT RESTRICTION

TEMPORARY COMMUNITY SUSPENSION
```

---

## ✦ Level `04` — Removal

Used for serious or repeated violations.

Possible action:

```text
REMOVAL FROM A SPACE

REMOVAL FROM AN ACTIVITY

COMMUNITY BAN

PROJECT PARTICIPATION RESTRICTION
```

---

## ✦ Level `05` — Immediate Permanent Action

Some behavior may justify immediate permanent removal without progressing
through every previous level.

Examples may include:

```text
CREDIBLE THREATS

SEVERE HARASSMENT

MALICIOUS DOXXING

SERIOUS SCAMS

MALWARE DISTRIBUTION

BAN EVASION AFTER SERIOUS ENFORCEMENT

DELIBERATE ATTACKS ON PROJECT SECURITY
```

---

## ✦ Enforcement Model

```text
INCIDENT
   │
   ▼
REVIEW
   │
   ▼
SEVERITY
   │
   ├── Minor ─────────► Guidance
   │
   ├── Moderate ──────► Warning
   │
   ├── Repeated ──────► Restriction
   │
   ├── Serious ───────► Removal
   │
   └── Severe ────────► Immediate Action
```

---

## ✦ Enforcement Is Not Always Sequential

The levels are not mandatory steps.

Severe misconduct may skip directly to stronger action.

---

## ✦ Content Removal

Gaming Horizon may remove or restrict content that violates project rules.

Removal of content does not necessarily mean the participant is permanently
banned.

---

## ✦ Repository Enforcement

On GitHub, actions may include:

```text
COMMENT MODERATION

ISSUE CLOSURE

LOCKING DISCUSSION

REJECTING A PULL REQUEST

LIMITING PARTICIPATION

BLOCKING AN ACCOUNT
```

depending on available platform controls and severity.

---

## ✦ Discord Enforcement

On Discord, actions may include:

```text
MESSAGE REMOVAL

WARNING

TIMEOUT

CHANNEL RESTRICTION

ROLE RESTRICTION

KICK

BAN
```

depending on severity and current server policy.

<a id="reporting"></a>

## ✦ Reporting Conduct Problems

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, use
an official Gaming Horizon reporting or support route.

Official community:

```text
https://discord.gg/M5PeNThBwF
```

Use the appropriate support or **Report a Member** process where available.

---

## ✦ Do Not Use Public Issues for Sensitive Reports

GitHub issues are public by default.

Do not post sensitive conduct reports containing:

```text
PRIVATE INFORMATION

PERSONAL IDENTIFIERS

PRIVATE SCREENSHOTS

SENSITIVE EVIDENCE
```

in a public repository issue merely to obtain moderator attention.

---

## ✦ What to Include

A useful report may contain:

```text
WHAT HAPPENED

WHERE IT HAPPENED

WHEN IT HAPPENED

WHO WAS INVOLVED

RELEVANT MESSAGE LINKS

SCREENSHOTS OR EVIDENCE

ANY IMMEDIATE SAFETY CONCERN
```

Only provide information relevant to the report.

---

## ✦ Evidence

Useful evidence may include:

```text
MESSAGE LINKS

ISSUE LINKS

PULL REQUEST COMMENTS

SCREENSHOTS

TIMESTAMPS

OTHER RELEVANT CONTEXT
```

Do not alter evidence in a misleading way.

---

## ✦ Preserve Privacy

Evidence may contain sensitive information.

Share only what is reasonably necessary for review.

---

## ✦ Report Flow

```text
INCIDENT
   │
   ▼
DOCUMENT RELEVANT DETAILS
   │
   ▼
USE OFFICIAL REPORT ROUTE
   │
   ▼
MODERATOR REVIEW
   │
   ▼
NEED MORE INFORMATION?
 ┌─┴───────────┐
 ▼             ▼
YES            NO
 │             │
 ▼             ▼
FOLLOW UP     DECIDE
               │
               ▼
             ACTION
```

---

## ✦ Emergency Situations

Gaming Horizon moderation is not an emergency service.

If there is an immediate real-world emergency, use the appropriate local
emergency services or trusted real-world support available to you.

---

<a id="report-confidentiality"></a>

## ✦ Report Confidentiality

Conduct reports should be handled with appropriate discretion.

Information may need to be shared with people responsible for reviewing or
resolving the incident.

The project should avoid unnecessary disclosure.

---

## ✦ Confidentiality Boundary

```text
REPORT
  │
  ▼
RELEVANT MODERATION TEAM
  │
  ▼
NEED-TO-KNOW REVIEW
  │
  ▼
DECISION
```

---

## ✦ No Guaranteed Absolute Confidentiality

Gaming Horizon should not promise absolute confidentiality in every situation.

Certain safety, platform, or legal circumstances may require information to be
shared appropriately.

---

<a id="false-reports"></a>

## ✦ False or Malicious Reports

Good-faith reports will not be penalized merely because moderators ultimately
decide no violation occurred.

However, knowingly fabricated reports may result in enforcement.

```text
GOOD-FAITH REPORT
      ≠
FALSE REPORT
```

A report can be mistaken without being malicious.

---

<a id="appeals"></a>

## ✦ Appeals

Moderation decisions may be appealable where an appeal process is available.

An appeal should explain:

```text
WHAT DECISION IS BEING APPEALED

WHY YOU BELIEVE IT SHOULD BE REVIEWED

WHAT CONTEXT MAY HAVE BEEN MISSED

WHAT HAS CHANGED, IF RELEVANT
```

---

## ✦ Appeal Standard

Appeals should be:

```text
RESPECTFUL

SPECIFIC

HONEST

NON-SPAMMING
```

---

## ✦ Appeal Flow

```text
MODERATION ACTION
       │
       ▼
APPEAL AVAILABLE?
   ┌───┴────┐
   ▼        ▼
  YES       NO
   │        │
   ▼        ▼
SUBMIT     ACTION
APPEAL     REMAINS
   │
   ▼
REVIEW
   │
   ▼
UPHOLD / MODIFY / REVERSE
```

---

## ✦ Appeal Abuse

Repeatedly submitting the same appeal without new information may be treated
as misuse of support systems.

---

<a id="maintainer-responsibilities"></a>

## ✦ Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers and moderators should:

- enforce this Code of Conduct consistently;
- avoid retaliation;
- avoid favoritism;
- protect sensitive reports;
- explain decisions when appropriate;
- correct mistakes when discovered;
- document serious moderation actions where appropriate;
- separate personal disagreement from policy enforcement;
- escalate serious safety or security concerns appropriately.

---

## ✦ Higher Standard for Project Representatives

People with project authority may have:

```text
MODERATION POWER

REPOSITORY ACCESS

COMMUNITY ACCESS

TRUSTED INFORMATION

PUBLIC VISIBILITY
```

That authority carries additional responsibility.

---

## ✦ Conflict of Interest

When practical, a moderator directly involved in a personal conflict should
avoid being the only person deciding the outcome.

---

## ✦ Moderator Misconduct

Moderators and maintainers are also subject to this Code of Conduct.

Official status does not provide an exemption.

---

```text
MORE ACTIVITY
     ≠
HEALTHIER COMMUNITY
```

A strong community is one where people can participate without unnecessary
risk or abuse.

---

## ✦ Community Safety Principles

```text
CLEAR RULES

SAFE REPORTING

PROPORTIONATE MODERATION

PRIVACY

APPEALS

ANTI-SPAM CONTROLS

ACCOUNTABILITY
```

---

<a id="privacy"></a>

## ✦ Privacy

unnecessary personal information.

Respect:

```text
PRIVATE ACCOUNTS

PRIVATE COMMUNICATION

PERSONAL INFORMATION

REPORTER CONFIDENTIALITY

EVIDENCE PRIVACY
```

See:

```text
PRIVACY.md
```

---

<a id="security"></a>

## ✦ Security

Security vulnerabilities should follow:

```text
SECURITY.md
```

Do not publish sensitive vulnerability details through ordinary conduct
reporting when the security-reporting process is more appropriate.

---

## ✦ Security vs Conduct

Example:

```text
MALICIOUS USER
      │
      ├── Harassment
      └── Security abuse
```

```text
MODERATION RESPONSE

SECURITY RESPONSE
```

---

<a id="accessibility"></a>

## ✦ Accessibility

Community accessibility includes more than interface design.

Communication should also aim to be:

```text
CLEAR

READABLE

UNDERSTANDABLE

PATIENT

NAVIGABLE
```

---

## ✦ Language and Communication

Not every participant will communicate with the same level of fluency,
technical vocabulary, or experience.

Avoid mocking people for:

```text
GRAMMAR

SPELLING

ACCENT

TECHNICAL EXPERIENCE

ASKING FOR CLARIFICATION
```

---

## ✦ Accessibility Is Not Special Treatment

Accessibility helps create equal opportunity to participate.

---

<a id="competitive-spaces"></a>

## ✦ Competitive Spaces

Competitive environments can become emotionally intense.

That does not remove conduct expectations.

Competition should allow:

```text
RIVALRY

CELEBRATION

FRIENDLY BANTER

COMPETITIVE ENERGY
```

without crossing into:

```text
HARASSMENT

THREATS

DISCRIMINATION

TARGETED HUMILIATION
```

## ✦ Creator Spaces

Creators should be able to share work without:

```text
PLAGIARISM

HARASSMENT

FALSE ATTRIBUTION

IMPERSONATION

UNAUTHORIZED REPOSTING CLAIMS
```

Creator-specific rights and platform rules may provide additional guidance.

See:

```text
docs/CREATORS.md
```

<a id="developer-spaces"></a>

## ✦ Developer Spaces

Developer discussion should encourage:

```text
REASONING

EVIDENCE

DOCUMENTATION

REVIEW

LEARNING
```

Technical expertise should not be used as a reason to demean newer
contributors.

See:

```text
docs/DEVELOPERS.md
```

---

<a id="ai-conduct"></a>

## ✦ AI and Automated Systems

AI or automation must not be used to scale misconduct.

Examples include:

```text
AUTOMATED HARASSMENT

MASS SPAM

IMPERSONATION

FABRICATED EVIDENCE

DECEPTIVE CONTENT

REPORT FLOODING
```

---

## ✦ AI-Generated Reports

AI-generated summaries or evidence descriptions should not be treated as
automatically accurate.

Relevant evidence should still be reviewed.

---

<a id="community-integrity"></a>

## ✦ Community Integrity

Healthy participation requires trust.

Avoid behavior designed to manipulate community perception through:

```text
FAKE ACCOUNTS

COORDINATED VOTE MANIPULATION

FABRICATED ENDORSEMENTS

FAKE STAFF CLAIMS

DECEPTIVE SCREENSHOTS

MANUFACTURED CONTROVERSY
```

---

## ✦ Good-Faith Participation

Gaming Horizon should welcome experimentation, mistakes, and disagreement when
participants act in good faith.

Bad-faith disruption is different.

```text
MISTAKE
    ≠
MALICIOUS ACTION
```

---

<a id="moderation-transparency"></a>

## ✦ Moderation Transparency

The project should aim to explain moderation standards publicly.

However, transparency should not require exposing:

```text
PRIVATE REPORTS

PRIVATE USER DATA

SECURITY DETAILS

CONFIDENTIAL EVIDENCE
```

---

## ✦ Public Transparency vs Individual Privacy

```text
PUBLIC RULES
      +
PRIVATE CASE HANDLING
      =
RESPONSIBLE MODERATION
```

<a id="enforcement-guidelines"></a>

## ✦ Enforcement Guidelines

Moderation should generally evaluate:

| Area | Question |
| --- | --- |
| **Severity** | How serious was the conduct? |
| **Impact** | Who or what was affected? |
| **Intent** | Does evidence suggest accident, negligence, or deliberate harm? |
| **Pattern** | Is this repeated behavior? |
| **History** | Were previous warnings issued? |
| **Safety** | Is immediate intervention required? |
| **Evidence** | What reliable information is available? |
| **Remediation** | Can the harm be corrected? |
| **Risk** | Could the behavior continue or escalate? |

---

## ✦ Moderation Decision Flow

```text
REPORT / OBSERVATION
        │
        ▼
VERIFY AVAILABLE EVIDENCE
        │
        ▼
POLICY VIOLATION?
    ┌───┴────┐
    ▼        ▼
   YES       NO
    │        │
    ▼        ▼
ASSESS      CLOSE /
SEVERITY    CLARIFY
    │
    ▼
SELECT PROPORTIONATE ACTION
    │
    ▼
DOCUMENT WHERE APPROPRIATE
    │
    ▼
APPEAL IF AVAILABLE
```

---

## ✦ Immediate Action

Moderators may act before completing a full investigation when necessary to
stop ongoing harm.

Temporary protective action does not always represent the final decision.

---

<a id="misuse"></a>

## ✦ Misuse of Community Systems

Community tools must be used for their intended purpose.

Examples of misuse include:

```text
TICKET FLOODING

FALSE REPORTS

BOT COMMAND ABUSE

MASS MENTIONS

SUPPORT SPAM

APPEAL SPAM

MODERATION EVASION
```

Serious misuse may result in immediate restriction or removal.

---

<a id="no-retaliation"></a>

## ✦ No Retaliation

Participants must not retaliate against someone for making a good-faith
conduct report.

Examples include:

```text
HARASSMENT AFTER REPORTING

THREATS

PUBLIC SHAMING

PRESSURE TO WITHDRAW A REPORT

TARGETED EXCLUSION
```

<a id="conduct-quality-standard"></a>

## ✦ Conduct Quality Standard

A healthy Gaming Horizon community should aim to be:

| # | Standard | Meaning |
|:---:|---|---|
| `01` | **Respectful** | People can disagree without personal attacks |
| `02` | **Constructive** | Discussion helps move work forward |
| `03` | **Safe** | Serious abuse is addressed |
| `04` | **Welcoming** | New participants can ask and learn |
| `05` | **Fair** | Moderation is not based on favoritism |
| `06` | **Private Where Needed** | Sensitive reports are handled carefully |
| `07` | **Accountable** | Project representatives follow the same standards |
| `08` | **Accessible** | Communication supports broad participation |
| `09` | **Evidence-Based** | Enforcement uses relevant information |
| `10` | **Proportionate** | Response matches severity and context |
| `11` | **Appealable Where Appropriate** | Decisions may be reviewed |
| `12` | **Evolvable** | Policy can improve with the community |

---

## ✦ Code of Conduct Golden Rules

### `01`

**Challenge ideas without attacking people.**

### `02`

**Respect personal boundaries and privacy.**

### `03`

**Harassment, threats, hate speech, doxxing, scams, and malicious disruption are not acceptable.**

### `04`

**Use reporting systems in good faith.**

### `05`

**Do not publicly expose sensitive evidence unnecessarily.**

### `06`

**Moderation should be proportionate, consistent, and evidence-based.**

### `07`

**Serious misconduct may result in immediate action.**

### `08`

**Good-faith criticism of Gaming Horizon is allowed.**

### `09`

**Moderators and maintainers are also accountable to this policy.**

### `10`

**Do not retaliate against people who make good-faith reports.**

### `11`

**Respectful disagreement is part of a healthy project.**

### `12`

**Protect the community so people can continue exploring beyond the Horizon.**
