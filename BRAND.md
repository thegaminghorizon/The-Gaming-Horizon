## ✦ Overview

The Gaming Horizon brand represents a premium, forward-looking gaming
ecosystem built around the idea that there is always something beyond the
player's current view.

The identity should communicate:

```text
DISCOVERY

POSSIBILITY

ADVENTURE

PROGRESS

COMMUNITY

GAMING

FUTURE
```

The visual system should feel modern and ambitious without becoming noisy,
artificial, inaccessible, or generic.

---

## ✦ Brand in One Sentence

> **Gaming Horizon is a premium browser-first gaming identity built around discovery, possibility, connection, and the belief that there is always another experience beyond the current Horizon.**

## ✦ Brand Personality

Gaming Horizon should feel:

| Quality | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Premium** | Refined, deliberate, polished |
| **Futuristic** | Forward-looking without becoming artificial |
| **Cinematic** | Atmospheric, dimensional, immersive |
| **Gaming-Focused** | Clearly connected to gaming culture and experiences |
| **Innovative** | Open to new ideas without chasing every trend |
| **Confident** | Strong without exaggeration |
| **Elegant** | Controlled rather than visually excessive |
| **Community-Driven** | People remain important to the ecosystem |
| **Accessible** | Visual ambition does not reduce usability |
| **International** | Presentation should work across a broad audience |
| **Trustworthy** | Truth matters more than promotional impact |

---

## ✦ Color Relationship

```text
DARK FOUNDATION
       │
       ▼
PURPLE / BLUE
       │
       ▼
CYAN ENERGY
       │
       ▼
SUBTLE VIOLET /
MAGENTA SUPPORT
       │
       ▼
FOCAL HIGHLIGHT
```

---

## ✦ Color Principle

Use color to establish hierarchy.

Do not make every element compete for attention.

---

## ✦ Color Restraint

Avoid:

```text
EVERY BUTTON NEON

EVERY BORDER GLOWING

EVERY TEXT BLOCK GRADIENT

TOO MANY ACCENT COLORS

EXTREME SATURATION EVERYWHERE
```

Premium presentation depends on restraint.

---

## ✦ Accent Hierarchy

A useful visual hierarchy may follow:

```text
PRIMARY ACCENT
      │
      └── Important action / identity


SECONDARY ACCENT
      │
      └── Supporting interaction


SOFT ACCENT
      │
      └── Background / atmosphere


NEUTRAL
      │
      └── Content / structural balance
```

---

## ✦ Light Theme

If a light visual treatment is used, it should remain recognizably
Gaming Horizon rather than simply reversing dark colors.

Maintain:

```text
PREMIUM CONTRAST

PURPLE / BLUE IDENTITY

CLEAR TYPOGRAPHY

CONTROLLED DEPTH

SUBTLE GLASS EFFECTS

ACCESSIBILITY
```

---

## ✦ Dark Theme

Dark presentation is strongly aligned with the current Gaming Horizon
identity.

However:

```text
DARK
   ≠
LOW CONTRAST
```

Text, controls, and interactive states must remain readable.

## ✦ Logo Geometry

The source geometry should not be manually reconstructed.

Do not:

```text
CHANGE ANGLES

MOVE ELEMENTS

CHANGE PROPORTIONS

ALTER SHAPE RELATIONSHIPS

SIMPLIFY WITHOUT APPROVAL

ADD RANDOM GEOMETRY
```

---

## ✦ Logo Proportions

Always preserve the original aspect ratio.

Avoid:

```text
STRETCHING

SQUASHING

SKEWING

PERSPECTIVE DISTORTION
```

---

## ✦ Logo Backgrounds

The logo should remain clear against its background.

Prefer backgrounds that provide sufficient visual separation.

Avoid placing it over:

```text
HIGHLY DETAILED IMAGERY

LOW-CONTRAST GRADIENTS

BUSY PARTICLE FIELDS

CLASHING COLORS
```

unless presentation treatment preserves readability.

---

## ✦ Logo Effects

Avoid unnecessary effects applied directly to the official mark:

```text
HEAVY DROP SHADOW

EXTREME OUTER GLOW

BEVEL

3D EXTRUSION

CHROME EFFECT

TEXTURE OVERLAY

RANDOM NEON OUTLINE
```

Environmental lighting may surround the logo in artwork, but the mark itself
should remain visually faithful.

---

## ✦ Logo Recoloring

Do not arbitrarily recolor the official source logo.

If future monochrome or alternate-color variants are needed, they should be
created and approved as official assets rather than improvised per use.

---

## ✦ Logo Placement

Give the logo enough surrounding space to remain visually independent.

This document intentionally does not invent a numeric clear-space formula.

Use visual judgment until a formal measured clear-space specification is
established.

---

## ✦ Minimum Logo Size

Do not define an arbitrary minimum pixel size without testing actual use.

The practical rule is:

```text
IF THE LOGO
IS NO LONGER CLEAR,
IT IS TOO SMALL.
```

Use dedicated icon assets for very small contexts.


---

## ✦ Typography Principle

Gaming Horizon typography should generally favor:

```text
CLEAN SANS-SERIF STRUCTURE

CLEAR DISPLAY HIERARCHY

STRONG HEADINGS

READABLE BODY TEXT

CONTROLLED LETTER SPACING

HIGH LEGIBILITY
```

---

## ✦ Font Selection

This brand document intentionally does not declare an official font family
without a maintained source decision.

Use typography that reflects the principles above until an official
typographic system is formally established.

---

## ✦ Display Typography

Display typography may be:

```text
BOLD

WIDE

SPACED

FUTURISTIC

STRUCTURED
```

but should remain readable.

---

## ✦ Body Typography

Body text should prioritize:

```text
READABILITY

COMFORTABLE LINE LENGTH

CLEAR CONTRAST

CONSISTENT SPACING
```

over visual experimentation.

---

## ✦ Letter Spacing

The brand may use expanded tracking selectively in identity lines such as:

```text
B E Y O N D   T H E   H O R I Z O N
```

Do not apply extreme spacing to long paragraphs or ordinary interface text.

---

```text
SYSTEM SUBSECTION
```

```text
TOO MANY FONT FAMILIES

UNREADABLE DISPLAY FONTS

ALL CAPS FOR LONG PARAGRAPHS

EXCESSIVE LETTER SPACING

TINY BODY TEXT

LOW-CONTRAST TEXT

DECORATIVE TEXT THAT LOOKS LIKE UI

```

## ✦ Borders

Borders should support structure rather than dominate it.

Preferred:

```text

SOFT

LOW-EMPHASIS

CONTEXTUAL
```

---

## ✦ Shadows

Shadows may create depth.

Prefer:

```text
SOFT DEPTH
```

over:

```text
HEAVY FLOATING BOX EFFECT
```

---

## ✦ Glow

Glow should be used as an accent.

```text
GLOW
   =
EMPHASIS
```

not:

```text
GLOW
   =
DEFAULT FOR EVERYTHING
```

<a id="motion"></a>

## ✦ Motion

Motion can reinforce the Horizon concept through:

```text
DEPTH

ENERGY

MOVEMENT

TRANSITION

DISCOVERY
```

---

## ✦ Motion Philosophy

Motion should answer:

```text
WHAT CHANGED?

WHERE DID IT MOVE?

WHAT SHOULD I NOTICE?

WHAT IS INTERACTIVE?
```

---

## ✦ Good Motion

Good motion may:

```text
SUPPORT NAVIGATION

REVEAL CONTENT

COMMUNICATE STATE

CREATE DEPTH

IMPROVE ORIENTATION

STRENGTHEN ATMOSPHERE
```

---

## ✦ Bad Motion

Avoid:

```text
CONSTANT UNRELATED MOVEMENT

DISTRACTING BACKGROUND EFFECTS

LONG BLOCKING ANIMATIONS

MOTION ON EVERY INTERACTION

FLASHING VISUALS

ANIMATION THAT HIDES CONTENT

ANIMATION THAT HURTS PERFORMANCE
```

---

## ✦ Reduced Motion

Reduced-motion preferences should be respected where relevant.

Conceptually:

```text
MOTION EXPERIENCE
      │
      ▼
USER PREFERS
REDUCED MOTION?
   ┌──┴──────┐
   ▼         ▼
  YES        NO
   │         │
   ▼         ▼
REDUCE     STANDARD
MOTION     MOTION
```

---

## ✦ Motion and Performance

```text
VISUAL EFFECT
      │
      ▼
CREATES VALUE?
  ┌───┴────┐
  ▼        ▼
 YES       NO
  │        │
  ▼        ▼
PERFORMANCE REMOVE
ACCEPTABLE?
```
