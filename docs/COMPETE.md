<a id="overview"></a>

## ✦ Overview

**Compete** represents the competitive-experience direction of
The Gaming Horizon.

Competition can create:

```text
CHALLENGE

PROGRESSION

TEAMWORK

MASTERY

COMMUNITY

DISCOVERY

RECOGNITION

SHARED EXPERIENCES
```

But competition only creates meaningful value when participants can understand
the rules, trust the result, and participate without unnecessary barriers.

The objective is therefore not:

```text
COMPETITION AT ANY COST
```

It is:

```text
SKILL
  +
FAIRNESS
  +
CLARITY
  +
ACCESSIBILITY
  +
COMMUNITY
  +
INTEGRITY
  =
MEANINGFUL COMPETITION
```


<a id="competition-principles"></a>

## ✦ Competition Principles

### `01` — Skill Before Manipulation

Competitive outcomes should reflect legitimate player or team performance
rather than deceptive mechanics.

---

### `02` — Fairness Before Engagement

Keeping users engaged is never a justification for unfair competition.

---

### `03` — Rules Before Results

Participants should understand the rules before being expected to follow them.

---

### `04` — Integrity Before Scale

A small trustworthy competition is more valuable than a large competition
people cannot trust.

---

### `05` — Accessibility Before Exclusion

Competitive interfaces should reduce unnecessary participation barriers.

---

### `06` — Community Before Hostility

Competition can be intense without encouraging harassment or abuse.

---

### `07` — Transparency Before Confusion

Scoring, qualification, penalties, progression, and important outcomes should
be understandable.

---

### `08` — Security Before Convenience

Competition systems should not weaken account, identity, or data protection.

---

### `09` — Learning Before Artificial Status

Competition should help participants improve rather than existing solely to
create arbitrary rankings.

---

### `10` — Evolution Before Rigidity

Formats and systems should evolve when evidence shows a better approach.

## ✦ Event Quality Gate

Before a structured competitive event:

- [ ] Purpose is clear
- [ ] Format is defined
- [ ] Rules are understandable
- [ ] Eligibility is defined
- [ ] Schedule is clear
- [ ] Scoring is defined
- [ ] Reporting path exists
- [ ] Dispute process exists where needed
- [ ] Accessibility has been considered
- [ ] Security has been considered
- [ ] Moderation expectations are clear
- [ ] Results can be verified appropriately
- [ ] Public claims are accurate

## ✦ Competitive Integrity

Competitive integrity means participants should be able to trust that outcomes
reflect the rules.

```text
                          INTEGRITY
                              │
         ┌────────────────────┼────────────────────┐
         ▼                    ▼                    ▼
      FAIR RULES        FAIR CONDITIONS      VERIFIED RESULTS
         │                    │                    │
         ├────────────────────┼────────────────────┤
         ▼                    ▼                    ▼
    ANTI-CHEATING          REPORTING            REVIEW
         │                    │                    │
         └────────────────────┼────────────────────┘
                              ▼
                           TRUST
```

---

## ✦ Fair Play

Fair play means participants should not intentionally gain prohibited
advantages.

Examples of integrity concerns may include:

```text
CHEATING

EXPLOIT ABUSE

RESULT MANIPULATION

IMPERSONATION

COLLUSION

UNAUTHORIZED ASSISTANCE

RULE EVASION
```

Specific definitions should be established by the relevant competition rules.

---

## ✦ Exploit Handling

Discovering a technical issue is different from intentionally abusing it.

Preferred flow:

```text
POTENTIAL EXPLOIT FOUND
          │
          ▼
STOP ABUSING IT
          │
          ▼
DOCUMENT SAFELY
          │
          ▼
REPORT
          │
          ▼
INVESTIGATE
```

Do not publicly spread sensitive exploit instructions when that could increase
abuse.

## ✦ Competition Golden Rules

### `01`

**Competition should reward legitimate performance.**

### `02`

**Rules should be understood before they are enforced.**

### `03`

**Fairness matters more than artificial engagement.**

### `04`

**Results should be explainable.**

### `05`

**Accessibility should not be treated as a competitive disadvantage.**

### `06`

**Competition never justifies harassment.**

### `07`

**AI assistance must respect competition rules.**

### `08`

**Security and integrity are part of competition architecture.**

### `09`

**Do not invent tournaments, rankings, participants, prizes, or statistics.**

### `10`

**Build competition that makes people want to improve — not distrust the system.**
