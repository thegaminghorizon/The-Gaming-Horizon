<a id="overview"></a>

## ✦ Overview

The Gaming Horizon Community is the people layer of the wider Gaming Horizon
ecosystem.

Technology can create a platform.

Content can create experiences.

But community creates connection.

The community system exists to help people:

```text
DISCOVER
CONNECT
DISCUSS
CREATE
SHARE
COMPETE
CONTRIBUTE
LEARN
GET SUPPORT
EXPLORE
```

The objective is not simply to create another gaming chat server.

The objective is to create a structured environment where people can
participate meaningfully across the Gaming Horizon ecosystem.

<a id="community-principles"></a>

## ✦ Community Principles

### `01` — Respect

Participation should remain respectful even when opinions differ.

---

### `02` — Belonging

New members should be able to understand where they are, what they can do, and
how they can participate.

---

### `03` — Safety

Community growth should never take priority over member safety.

---

### `04` — Clarity

Rules, roles, support paths, moderation systems, and official announcements
should remain understandable.

---

### `05` — Participation

Members should have meaningful ways to participate beyond passive reading.

---

### `06` — Creativity

Creators, builders, players, and contributors should have space to share ideas
without drowning the community in uncontrolled promotion.

---

### `07` — Contribution

Community participation can extend into documentation, feedback, GitHub,
testing, and ecosystem improvement.

---

### `08` — Accessibility

Community information should be organized so people can actually find and
understand it.

---

### `09` — Accountability

Rules should apply consistently.

---

### `10` — Evolution

The community system should improve as Gaming Horizon learns what members
actually need.

It should not be treated as entertainment, status, or personal authority.

```text
RULE / INCIDENT
      │
      ▼
AVAILABLE EVIDENCE
      │
      ▼
CONTEXT
      │
      ▼
MODERATION REVIEW
      │
      ▼
DECISION
      │
      ▼
ACTION / NO ACTION
```

## ✦ Moderation Principles

Moderation should aim for:

```text
CONSISTENCY

PROPORTIONALITY

EVIDENCE

CONTEXT

PRIVACY

CLEAR COMMUNICATION

DOCUMENTED RULES
```
