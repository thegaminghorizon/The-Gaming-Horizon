## ✦ Overview

**Creators** represent the expressive and creative layer of
The Gaming Horizon ecosystem.

Gaming is not only something people play.

It is also something people:

```text
DISCUSS

ANALYZE

DESIGN

DOCUMENT

TEACH

REIMAGINE

BUILD AROUND

SHARE

CELEBRATE
```

The creator direction exists to explore how Gaming Horizon can support those
activities without turning creativity into a purely promotional or
algorithm-driven system.

---

The long-term creator vision is centered on a simple idea:

> **Give people useful ways to create around gaming while preserving human judgment, originality, accessibility, community value, and creative identity.**

creation, discovery, sharing, collaboration, documentation, and community

<a id="creator-principles"></a>

## ✦ Creator Principles

### `01` — Human Creativity First

AI and tools may assist creation.

They should not erase human intent, judgment, or responsibility.

---

### `02` — Purpose Before Volume

More content is not automatically better.

Useful creation is more valuable than endless output.

---

### `03` — Discovery Without Manipulation

Creator discovery should help people find relevant work without encouraging
deceptive engagement tactics.

---

### `04` — Community Before Spam

Creator participation should strengthen community rather than turn community
spaces into advertisement feeds.

---

### `05` — Authenticity Before Artificial Authority

Creators should not misrepresent unofficial content as official Gaming Horizon
material.

---

### `06` — Accessibility Before Spectacle

Creative experiences should remain understandable and usable.

---

### `07` — Collaboration Before Isolation

Creators should be able to learn from and collaborate with others where useful.

---

### `08` — Rights Before Convenience

Creators should consider ownership, permission, attribution, and third-party
rights.

---

### `09` — Security Before Integration

Creator tooling should not expose credentials or weaken platform security.

---

### `10` — Evolution Before Rigidity

The creator ecosystem should evolve as new creative formats and technologies
emerge.

## ✦ Creator Quality Standard

A Gaming Horizon creator experience should aim to be:

| # | Standard | Meaning |
|:---:|---|---|
| `01` | **Human-Led** | Creators retain judgment and identity |
| `02` | **Creative** | Supports meaningful expression |
| `03` | **Purposeful** | Tools solve real creator needs |
| `04` | **Discoverable** | Useful work can be found |
| `05` | **Community-Friendly** | Sharing strengthens participation |
| `06` | **Accessible** | Creation and consumption reduce barriers |
| `07` | **Rights-Aware** | Ownership and attribution are respected |
| `08` | **Secure** | Accounts, uploads, and integrations are protected |
| `09` | **Private** | Public and private information remain distinct |
| `10` | **Reliable** | Creator work is not unnecessarily lost |
| `11` | **Truthful** | Official and creator content remain distinct |
| `12` | **Evolvable** | New creative formats can emerge |

---

## ✦ Creator Golden Rules

### `01`

**Creators should remain creators — tools should support them, not replace them.**

### `02`

**Create value, not just volume.**

### `03`

**Discovery should not require spam.**

### `04`

**Community participation should not become uncontrolled advertising.**

### `05`

**Respect ownership, permission, and attribution.**

### `06`

**AI output should be reviewed before it becomes creator output.**

### `07`

**Accessibility belongs inside the creative workflow.**

### `08`

**Public creator identity should remain separate from private account data.**

### `09`

**Never invent creator programs, partnerships, metrics, payouts, or sponsorships.**

### `10`

**Build tools that expand creative possibility without narrowing creative identity.**
