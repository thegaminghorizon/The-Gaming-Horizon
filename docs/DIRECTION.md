It sits between:

```text
VISION
```

and:

```text
DEVELOPMENT
```


```text
HOW DO WE BUILD IT?
```

```text
INTEROPERABILITY

SHAREABLE EXPERIENCES
```

<a id="direction-principles"></a>

## ✦ Direction Principles

### `01` — Gaming First

Every major system should reinforce the gaming ecosystem.

Gaming Horizon should not lose its identity by becoming a collection of
unrelated technologies.

---

### `02` — Purpose Before Features

The project should not measure progress by feature count.

Every feature should answer:

```text
WHAT PROBLEM DOES THIS SOLVE?
```

---

### `03` — Experience Before Complexity

A technically impressive system is not useful if the experience becomes worse.

---

### `04` — Browser Before Friction

The web should remain a strength rather than an obstacle.

---

### `05` — Community Before Isolation

Gaming is often more meaningful when people can connect around it.

---

### `06` — Creativity Before Content Volume

Creators should be able to create meaningful value without being pushed toward
spam or endless output.

---

### `07` — Fairness Before Competitive Manipulation

Competition should be understandable and trustworthy.

---

### `08` — Developer Clarity Before Platform Expansion

Developer-facing systems should become public only when they are stable enough
to create real value.

---

### `09` — AI With Purpose

Artificial intelligence should solve real problems rather than exist as a
marketing label.

---

### `10` — Accessibility by Default

Access should be considered across the entire ecosystem.

---

### `11` — Trust Before Growth

Security, privacy, transparency, and reliability matter more than artificial
scale.

---

### `12` — Evolution Without Losing Identity

Gaming Horizon should evolve without abandoning the principles that define it.

---

- [ ] Privacy implications were considered

- [ ] Community impact was considered
